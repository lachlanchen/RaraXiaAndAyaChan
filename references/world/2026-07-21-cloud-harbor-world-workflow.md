# Cloud Harbor World Workflow

## Series Foundation

The current LALACHAN world is **云港万象**. 啦啦侠、阿芽酱、飒飒君 and 庄子机器人 share 云港小屋 and travel through a connected network of familiar places. The stable home, recurring tools, distinctive relationships, and slowly unfolding mystery make individual shorts feel like episodes of one series rather than unrelated prompts.

The first open thread is **正在褪色的旅印**: old journey stamps in the patchwork notebook are fading, while a blue-tailed paper bird appears near missing routes. Each episode must still solve its own immediate problem.
