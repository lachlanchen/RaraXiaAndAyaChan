# Natural and Funny 15-Second Story Pattern

Date: `2026-07-15`

This guide distills why `草垫子真的飞起来了` feels clearer, warmer, and
funnier than many earlier LALACHAN drafts. Reuse the story mechanics, not the
grass-sliding setting or its exact punchline.

## Core Shape

A strong 15-second story usually needs only:

```text
one place + one activity + one physical problem + two visible reactions + one payoff
```

Recommended sequence:

```text
enter the action
-> show one caring gesture
-> let a physical action trigger movement
-> escalate through two visual jokes
-> stop safely
-> end with a short character-based punchline
```

Do not add lore, a villain, a lesson, or another gadget when the activity
already supplies enough tension.

## Why The Qiandaohu Story Works

### 1. It starts inside the scene

The characters are already sitting on the grass mat. There is no explanation
of why they came, what the attraction means, or what they will learn.

### 2. Affection is shown, not announced

阿芽酱 grabs 啦啦侠's arm; 啦啦侠 gives her his hand and asks her to sit
closer. This creates a couple feeling through visible behavior. The narrator
never says they are romantic.

### 3. Every beat has a visible cause

```text
飒飒君 lifts his feet
-> the mat starts moving
-> it hits a bump
-> 阿芽酱 leans into 啦啦侠
-> 飒飒君 bounces and lands back in place
-> the mat spins at the bottom
-> 啦啦侠 feels the whole lake is spinning
```

Nothing happens merely because the script says `突然`.

### 4. The comedy escalates naturally

The scene uses three increasingly strong jokes:

1. The ride launches before 飒飒君 can count to three.
2. He bounces into the air and accidentally lands correctly.
3. 啦啦侠 asks the lake to stop spinning before another ride.

The last line pays off the motion viewers just saw. It is not a slogan or a
separate joke pasted onto the ending.

### 5. Dialogue is short and speakable

Each line can be said naturally while moving. No character explains the theme,
reports a status, or speaks like a generation prompt.

### 6. Warmth survives the conflict

The danger is small and safe. Nobody scolds anyone. Surprise becomes laughter,
and 阿芽酱 immediately wants another turn. The result is happy rather than
instructional or parent-like.

## Character Jobs

Not every character must speak. Give each visible character one useful job.

- **啦啦侠:** reassures a friend, reacts sincerely, and lands the concrete
  final joke.
- **阿芽酱:** notices what matters, expresses trust through action, and keeps
  the emotional tone warm.
- **飒飒君:** triggers movement or supplies physical comedy.
- **庄子:** handles a practical detail or gives one dry observation only when
  it improves the scene. He may remain silent rather than forcing a line.

Recurring props follow the same rule: one prop, one job. A word card can match
the episode's action, but it remains a physical background object and does not
interrupt the story.

## Drafting Template

```text
[具体地点]，[一眼能看懂的路线或环境]。四人刚[开始一项活动]，
[角色A]就[一个自然的小动作，表现紧张、信任或期待]。

[角色B]：“[简短、日常的回应]。”

[角色C]刚[触发动作]，[主要道具]立刻[开始运动]。

“[来不及准备的短反应]！”

[运动中的第一个意外]，让[角色A和B产生关系动作]；
[第二个意外]，让[角色C完成一个看得见的身体笑点]。

[道具安全停下]。

[角色A]：“[还想再来、终于拿到、或完成目标]！”

[角色B]：“[只针对刚才物理现象的具体误解或夸张反应]。”
```

## Dialogue Test

Read every line aloud. Keep it only if all answers are yes:

- Could someone really say this while running, eating, falling, or laughing?
- Does the line react to something visible in the same shot?
- Can the audience understand it immediately without lore?
- Does it sound different from the other characters' lines?
- Is it shorter and plainer than the first version?

Prefer three spoken beats in 15 seconds. Silence, expressions, and movement
should carry the rest.

## Hard Rejections

Rewrite the draft if it contains any of these:

- abstract conclusions such as `真正的`, `意义`, `勇气值`, or `系统提示`;
- narration saying the scene is funny, warm, romantic, or cute;
- uncaused magic or repeated `突然`;
- a moral added after the real ending;
- forced dialogue for all four characters;
- parent-like scolding such as `先坐好，再可爱`;
- several villains, props, concepts, or twists competing in 15 seconds;
- production language mixed into the story;
- a punchline unrelated to the action viewers just watched.

## Eight-Point Gate

Before accepting a draft, verify:

1. The whole story is retellable in one sentence.
2. The main activity starts within the first two seconds.
3. Every event is caused by the previous event.
4. There is one small caring or relationship gesture.
5. The central joke is visible even with the sound off.
6. Dialogue sounds like normal spoken Chinese.
7. The final line pays off the physical action.
8. Removing any extra element would not hurt the story; if it would not, remove
   that element.

## Paste-Ready Writing Instruction

```text
写一个15秒的LALACHAN小故事。只选一个具体地点和一项正在发生的活动，
从动作开始，不解释背景。让一个自然的小动作表现角色之间的关心，不要直接
说明感情。用一个动作引发后续事件，再安排两个由同一运动过程产生的视觉笑点。
最多三组短台词，使用日常、能直接说出口的中文，不讲道理，不写口号，不强迫
每个角色开口。结尾必须回应刚发生的物理现象，用具体、略夸张但马上能听懂的
角色反应收尾。写完后逐句检查因果、口语和多余元素，再输出成稿。
```

For a 30-second version, add one failed attempt or one meaningful choice. Do
not double the cast, lore, props, or lessons.
